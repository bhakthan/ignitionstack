# SiteSync — Smart Construction Project Management Platform

## Overview
Build a construction project management platform for Apex Builders Group (fictitious), a commercial construction firm managing 40+ active job sites with $1.2B in annual contracts spanning office towers, hospitals, and mixed-use developments.

## Core Features

### Project Command Center
- Real-time project timeline with baseline vs. actual tracking
- Budget burn-down dashboards with cost-to-complete forecasting
- Weather impact analysis and schedule auto-adjustment
- Multi-project portfolio view with red/amber/green status

### Building Information Modeling (BIM) Integration
- 3D model viewer with issue markup and clash detection results
- RFI (Request for Information) management linked to model elements
- Change order tracking with cost and schedule impact analysis
- As-built documentation linked to BIM components

### Safety & Compliance Agent
- Daily safety checklist generation based on active trades and weather
- OSHA compliance monitoring with automatic citation risk scoring
- Incident reporting with root cause analysis workflow
- Subcontractor safety score tracking and prequalification

### Field Operations Mobile App
- Daily log entry with photo/video capture and GPS tagging
- Punch list management with trade assignment and due dates
- Material delivery tracking and storage location mapping
- Time card entry with geo-fencing verification

### Document Management
- Auto-classification of submittals, RFIs, change orders, and drawings
- Version control with redline comparison
- Approval workflow engine with escalation rules
- Full-text search with RAG-powered Q&A over project documents

## Non-Functional Requirements
- Offline-capable mobile app (sync when connectivity restored)
- Support 500+ concurrent field users across 40 sites
- Document storage: 10TB+ with 10-year retention
- Real-time notifications: < 5 second delivery for safety alerts
- 99.9% uptime during business hours (6 AM – 8 PM local)

## User Roles
- Project Manager (schedules, budgets, RFIs, change orders)
- Superintendent (daily logs, field coordination, safety)
- Safety Director (compliance, incidents, training)
- Estimator (cost tracking, change order analysis)
- Subcontractor (daily logs, punch lists, time cards)
- Owner/Client (read-only dashboard, milestone tracking)

## Technical Constraints
- BIM integration via IFC (Industry Foundation Classes) open standard
- Accounting system integration: Sage 300 CRE or Viewpoint Vista
- Scheduling integration: Primavera P6 or Microsoft Project
- Must support low-bandwidth satellite connections on remote sites
- Data residency: US and Canada regions
